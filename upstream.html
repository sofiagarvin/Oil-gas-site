Upstream (Exploration & Production)

Upstream covers everything from securing acreage to drilling wells and producing oil and gas. It is defined by subsurface uncertainty, steep decline curves, and capital discipline.

From acreage to barrels

  • Lease & title: secure mineral rights and surface access.
  • Evaluate rock: geologic mapping, seismic, type curves.
  • Drill: build the wellbore to target depth and trajectory.
  • Complete: perforate and hydraulically fracture to create flow paths.
  • Produce: lift, separate, treat; manage declines and downtime.
  • Book reserves: estimate recoverable volumes and classify certainty.
Economics
Value drivers

How upstream makes money

  • Revenue: volumes × realized price (after differentials, transport, quality).
  • Costs: drilling & completions (capex), LOE, workovers, taxes.
  • Decline curves: production drops over time; early rates matter for IRR.
  • Capital efficiency: $/boe, $/ft, $/stage; cycle time and learning curves.
  • Risk management: hedging, diversification, and operational reliability.

Quick mental model
One sentence

Upstream = manufacturing + geology

  • Commodity “factory” where each well is a project with uncertain input quality (the rock).
  • Operational excellence improves repeatability; subsurface learning improves targeting.
  • Financial discipline determines whether growth creates or destroys value.
