About this website

This website serves to explain the key operational stages of the oil and gas industry, as well as provide a terminology page to help people understand key terms and definitions in the industry.

The chatbot streamlines the process of finding the definition of a specific word and understanding its context.

At a glance
What you’ll find Upstream, midstream, and downstream summaries + a searchable terminology reference.
How to use it Start with the value chain pages, then use the terminology page (and chatbot) to clarify concepts quickly.
Goal Make oil & gas operations and language easier to understand—fast.
Purpose

Clear structure, fast definitions

The site is designed to provide a simple mental model of how hydrocarbons move from rock to market, and to reduce friction when encountering unfamiliar terms.

  • Operational stages: concise explanations of upstream, midstream, and downstream.
  • Terminology: a reference page for key terms and why they matter.
  • Chatbot support: quickly find definitions and understand context without digging through documents.
